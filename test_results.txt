Changes made before this run, to match the PDF:
- The outcomes were renamed to the PDF's three: Concern supported, Benign
  explanation supported, Unresolved. Every case now starts as Unresolved.
  (On 23 Oct "Benign" was reworded in the app as "Harmless explanation
  supported", at the student's request; the checks were re-run afterwards.)
- Closing now requires a reason, a next step and at least one evidence note.
  An Unresolved case can close only after a note explains the missing
  information and the next step. If anything is missing, the app says what to
  add (previously the Close button was simply disabled).
- History entries for decisions now show the previous and new outcome, reason
  and next step. The history is shown in time order, oldest first (it was
  newest first).

          cases; the four fields match the file; every case starts New and
          Unresolved.

          message and context. Cases previously started with no outcome; they

          "none of the 1 report ... was imported").
